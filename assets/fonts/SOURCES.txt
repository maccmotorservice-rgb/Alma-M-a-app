Build: FontTools 4.61.1 + Brotli 1.2.0. Latin subset includes Spanish accents,
inverted question/exclamation marks, Latin-1, standard punctuation, euro sign,
arrows and mathematical minus. Manrope retains a variable wght axis 400-800
(default 400); Instrument Serif regular and italic are static weight 400.
FontTools subsets precede Manrope axis instantiation. Names were updated from
the upstream ExtraLight default to Regular using updateFontNames=True.

Suggested CSS font-family names: "Manrope" and "Instrument Serif".
Manrope: font-style normal; font-weight 400 800.
Instrument Serif: separate normal and italic faces, font-weight 400.
Use font-display: swap. No network requests to Google Fonts are needed.
